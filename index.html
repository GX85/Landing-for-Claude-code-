FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Focus?

An AI assistant by Team Swift that answers like a smart, funny friend and builds things on request. It has three tiers: Storm for speed, GEN for everyday work and Clorus for deep thinking and building. Aeronus, the agent hub, is there when you want several AIs on one task.

Can it really build games, websites and presentations?

Yes. Ask for one and Focus writes the whole thing, runs it in a sandbox, fixes the errors it finds and shows it working in the chat. Open it full screen or download the file. Presentations use a designed slide template, so they always look sharp.

Is Focus free?

Yes. The built-in free models and Focus Image work without a key. For Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, video generators and agents you add your own keys and pay those providers directly — Focus adds nothing on top.

Where are my API keys stored?

Only in your browser. When an agent runs, its key travels with that one request through the Focus proxy to the provider and isn't saved anywhere on the server.

Which models can I use?

Claude, GPT, Gemini and Grok with your own keys; hundreds more through OpenRouter (free ones included) or any OpenAI-compatible API; and local models on your computer with Ollama.

Can Focus make videos and images?

Yes. Connect Gemini (Veo, Imagen), Luma, Replicate, Pollinations or OpenAI images and add a generator to your team. In Relay mode a chat model writes the scene and the generator renders it.

What are autonomous agents?

Agents like Manus, Jules and Devin work on their own for minutes or hours — browsing, writing code, opening pull requests. Focus starts the task, follows it and shows the result in your chat.

What are skills?

Skills are short sets of instructions an agent always follows, like “Fact-check” or “Match my language”. Focus has ten built in; you can write your own on the Agents page and turn them on for any agent.

Do I need an account?

Not to try it. When sign-in is switched on you can use GitHub, Google or your email — we send a one-time code, no password to remember.

Do I need to install anything?

No — Focus runs in your browser. Ollama is optional, for running models on your own computer.

Who makes Focus?

Focus is built by Team Swift.
